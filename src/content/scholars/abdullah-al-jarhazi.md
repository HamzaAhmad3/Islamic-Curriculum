Abd Allah ibn Sulayman al-Jarhazi al-Zabidi was a later Shafi'i jurist known for his commentary on al-Manhaj al-Qawim bi Sharh Masa'il al-Ta'lim by Ibn Hajar al-Haytami. His hashiyah was written as part of the long scholarly tradition of annotating Ibn Hajar's works, with generations of Shafi'i jurists contributing explanatory marginalia that clarified difficult passages, discussed legal details, and expanded upon the author's arguments.

Among the many hashiyahs written on al-Manhaj al-Qawim, al-Jarhazi's became distinguished for its careful verification and practical usefulness. Rather than merely explaining the text, he frequently identifies the mu'tamad (relied-upon) position within the Shafi'i school when multiple opinions exist, often mentioning the reasoning or authorities supporting that position. This approach made the work especially valuable for students seeking to determine the authoritative ruling of the madhhab.

His commentary is also recognized for preserving valuable quotations and transmitting discussions not readily found in more commonly studied works. It reflects a method of precise scholarship, careful investigation, and attention to legal detail, making it an important reference alongside other marginal commentaries on al-Manhaj al-Qawim. Although the various hashiyahs on the text differ in style and purpose, al-Jarhazi's is particularly appreciated for its emphasis on identifying the authoritative opinion of the school, a feature that has made it especially beneficial for students and researchers of Shafi'i jurisprudence.
